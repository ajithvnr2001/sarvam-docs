## Your questions, answered

### How many Gujarati voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers multiple Gujarati voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control and temperature adjustment.

### Does it handle Gujlish code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 handles natural Gujarati-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or unnatural pauses between languages.

### How does it handle Gujarati aspirated consonants?

Our phoneme model is trained to correctly produce the subtle but critical distinction between aspirated and unaspirated stops in Gujarati.

### Can I use Gujarati TTS for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Gujarati, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Gujarati TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz.
