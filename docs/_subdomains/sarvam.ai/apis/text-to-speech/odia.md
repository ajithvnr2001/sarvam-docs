## Your questions, answered

### How many Odia voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers multiple Odia voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control and temperature adjustment.

### Does it handle Odia-English code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 handles natural Odia-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or unnatural pauses between languages.

### How does it handle Odia retroflex consonants?

Our phoneme model is trained on Odia-specific retroflex articulation patterns, producing sounds critical for intelligibility that generic models routinely miss.

### Can I use Odia TTS for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Odia, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Odia TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz.
