## About the Role

**Sarvam Studio** is our media platform — spanning AI dubbing, live translation, text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and audiobook & podcast generation, powered by a shared foundation of voice cloning, stem separation and music generation. Its users range from individual creators to media houses to large broadcasters and streaming platforms.

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager who knows the media and entertainment industry from the inside — someone **who has built products for OTT, broadcast, streaming, or media-tech platforms and understands how content actually gets made, localized, distributed, and monetized**. You'll own how Studio serves the media industry: what a dubbing pipeline needs to look like for a broadcaster running hundreds of hours of content a month, what "broadcast quality" means for AI-generated voice, and how AI reshapes the economics of content creation and localization.

This is a builder's role, not a strategy-deck role. You'll talk to studios, broadcasters, and creators constantly, ship product every week, and get deep into how our voice and media models behave in production.

## What You'll Do

  * Own the Studio product roadmap for media & entertainment customers end-to-end — from discovery with broadcasters, OTT platforms, and content studios, to specs, shipping, and measuring outcomes

  * Translate media workflows into product: content ingestion, dubbing and localization pipelines, QC and human-in-the-loop review, delivery formats, and rights/compliance requirements that broadcast and streaming customers demand

  * Define what "good" means for AI-generated media — voice quality, lip-sync, emotional nuance, translation fidelity — and build the eval pipelines and feedback loops that hold the product to it

  * Get into the details of how AI systems behave in production: model selection, prompt and pipeline design, latency and cost budgets, failure modes

  * Work with the GTM team on pricing, packaging, and positioning for media segments — from per-minute creator pricing to enterprise localization contracts

  * Be the voice of the media customer internally: relay precise product and model feedback (dubbing failures, audio artefacts, translation misses) to research, and close the loop with customers

  * Track the AI-in-media landscape — content creation, localization, and playout platforms — and bring a sharp point of view on where Studio should build, partner, or pass

  * Ship product every week, not plans




## What We're Looking For

  * 5–10 years of product management experience, with real products shipped at meaningful scale

  * Strong preference for experience in media & entertainment — Video & Content first platforms OTT/streaming platforms (Prime Video, JioHotstar, Zee5, SonyLIV), broadcasters, studios, or media-tech/content-supply-chain companies (Adobe, Amagi, Prime Focus, or similar)

  * Deep understanding of the content lifecycle: how content is produced, transformed (dubbing, subtitling), packaged, distributed, and monetized across broadcast and streaming

  * Hands-on familiarity with generative AI in content workflows — voice synthesis, dubbing, video/audio generation — and clear thinking on quality vs. cost vs. speed trade-offs

  * Strong data skills: comfortable writing SQL, setting up funnels, and defining success metrics that matter (not vanity numbers)

  * Strong product thinking: you start with the customer's problem, structure vague problem spaces, and ship v1 without over-engineering

  * High ownership, high agency: you don't wait for permission, and you've stayed up to fix something because you couldn't let it stay broken

  * Clear communicator — equally effective with a dubbing director, an enterprise buyer, and a research engineer




## Bonus Points
