**About the Role**

We are looking for a **Staff Software Engineer - Frontend** to help us solve some of the hardest frontend problems as we build AI products at scale.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You will work on problems involving real-time systems, streaming, performance, reliability, complex user workflows, and large-scale frontend architecture. You will also help shape the way frontend engineering is done across the organization.   


## What you'll do

  * Own the frontend architecture for complex, high-scale applications.

  * Solve challenging problems around real-time interactions, streaming data, asynchronous workflows, and continuously changing UI state.

  * Build reliable experiences around AI-generated responses, long-running tasks, tool calls, and partial results.

  * Design systems that remain responsive and reliable when dealing with large amounts of data and long-running operations.

  * Work closely with backend, product, and design teams to solve problems end to end.

  * Identify architectural bottlenecks before they become scaling problems and drive the right long-term solutions.

  * Improve frontend performance across loading, rendering, network usage, memory, and bundle size.

  * Build reusable frontend infrastructure, components, and patterns that can be adopted across teams.

  * Improve observability and production debugging through metrics, logging, error tracking, traces, and monitoring.

  * Own complex production issues, identify root causes, and make sure the same problems don't keep coming back.

  * Establish better approaches to testing, releases, feature flags, CI/CD, and frontend reliability.

  * Lead architecture discussions and technical decisions across teams.

  * Mentor engineers and help raise the overall frontend engineering bar.

  * Take ambiguous problems from problem definition → design → implementation → production.




## What we're looking for

  * 8+ years of software engineering experience, with strong hands-on frontend experience.

  * Deep experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Next.js.

  * Strong understanding of frontend architecture, browser fundamentals, networking, rendering, and the web platform.

  * Experience designing and scaling large production frontend applications.

  * Strong understanding of state management, APIs, caching, authentication, and asynchronous systems.

  * Experience building real-time or streaming experiences using technologies such as WebSockets, SSE, or streaming APIs.

  * Strong understanding of web performance and experience solving real production performance problems.

  * Experience with frontend observability and debugging production issues.

  * Experience building shared components, design systems, or frontend platforms.

  * Strong system-design skills and the ability to make practical architectural trade-offs.

  * Ability to influence technical decisions across teams without relying on formal authority.

  * Strong ownership and a bias toward solving problems at their root.

  * Understanding of video and audio fundamentals, including media processing, encoding/decoding, formats, streaming, synchronization, and handling media workflows at scale.

  * Experience building frontend experiences for video/audio processing or dubbing workflows is highly valuable.

  * Understanding of document digitization workflows, including document upload, processing states, previews, extraction results, editing/review experiences, and handling large or complex documents.

  * Experience building frontend applications that deal with large files, long-running processing jobs, progress tracking, retries, failures, and partial results.
