## About the Role

Engagement Manager is a senior client-facing role in the vertical. You own pipeline development, account strategy, and long-term engagement management. This is a builder role for someone who understands the procurement landscape, can engage at the CXO and technical leadership level, and can convert complex AI capability into funded, deployable programmes.

## What You'll Do

  * Own the day-to-day management of active client engagements, from scoping and alignment through to operational handover and adoption
  * Serve as the primary point of contact for clients across the engagement lifecycle; manage expectations proactively and honestly
  * Translate client operational problems into precise briefs that deployment and engineering teams can act on
  * Track delivery against commitments; surface risks early and drive resolution without waiting for escalation
  * Work with Strategic Deployment Engineers to ensure what is being built reflects what the client actually needs
  * Support pipeline development: account mapping, stakeholder outreach, and PoC scoping
  * Build and maintain working relationships with mid-to-senior client stakeholders; grow account depth over time
  * Surface ground-level observations back to the vertical as market intelligence



## What We're Looking For

  * 4–6 years of professional experience; foundational background at a top-tier consulting firm strongly preferred — with a track record of owning complex analytical work, structuring ambiguous problems, and being accountable for outcomes
  * At least 2 years in strategic or complex enterprise GTM, account management, or programme delivery — where you owned client relationships, not just supported them
  * Comfort operating in long-cycle, relationship-driven environments where trust is built over months, not deals
  * Strong written and verbal communication, able to hold a room with senior stakeholders and write briefs that people act on
  * Familiarity with GenAI or ML concepts sufficient to engage credibly with technical teams and translate capability to clients
  * Experience in or adjacent to defence, government, critical infrastructure and complex enterprises is a plus
