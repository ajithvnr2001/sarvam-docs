## **About the Role**

We’re looking for a Marketing Intern to help execute high-priority initiatives across Sarvam’s marketing function. You will work on a varied mix of market research, campaigns, product marketing, events and content, collaborating closely with teams across Marketing, Product, GTM and Design.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can bring structure to open-ended problems, move quickly from idea to execution and maintain a high bar for quality. Your work will contribute directly to how Sarvam takes its products to market, engages enterprise and developer audiences, and shows up across digital and in-person channels.

## **What You’ll Do**

  * Build campaigns across content, search, paid and community channels, from audience and messaging to execution and measurement.

  * Work with Product, GTM and Marketing on launches, product assets and new growth initiatives.

  * Plan and execute conferences, roundtables, meetups and other offline experiences across vendors, logistics, communication and on-ground delivery.

  * Help grow developer and enterprise communities through events, partnerships, webinars and ongoing engagement.

  * Improve Sarvam’s discoverability across SEO, content and AI-powered search.

  * Conduct market, customer and competitive research, turning findings into clear briefs and recommendations.




## **What We’re Looking For**

  * High agency and execution ability, with a track record of taking ownership and delivering high-quality work.

  * Strong organisation and follow-through; calm and detail-oriented under deadline pressure.

  * Clear writing and structured thinking, with the ability to communicate ideas simply.

  * Strong growth instincts across audience, message, channel, experimentation and measurement.

  * Fluency with modern AI tools, with the ability to build workflows and automations that improve research, content, analysis and execution.

  * Comfort with marketing tools such as CRM, email and basic analytics




## **Good to Have**

  * Experience running a campus event, community, newsletter, side project or a small business.

  * Familiarity with growth channels such as email, SEO, paid acquisition, content, partnerships and community.

  * Familiarity with Canva, Figma, Notion, HubSpot, analytics tools or basic video editing.




If you have work samples that demonstrate strong writing, structured thinking or execution, include them with your application.
