## Your questions, answered.

### How can my team get access?

Indus Cowork is not generally available yet. Talk to us about your team’s workflows and access to the product.

### How is Indus Cowork different from a chat assistant?

A chat assistant answers a question. A Cowork teammate plans the work, researches and acts inside your connected apps. It reviews its output and brings the result back for your approval.

### Which apps can a teammate connect to?

There are more than 100 connectors to popular apps, covering productivity, data, and engineering tools. You can also bring your own connector for anything custom.

### Can I create my own skills?

Yes. Create as many skills as you need. There is a skill-creator skill too, so you can use AI to build new skills for your own purpose.

### How do I work with a teammate day to day?

Teammates connect to Slack or Indus Chat. You message them like any colleague, and they can also run scheduled tasks on their own.

### Do I stay in control of what a teammate does?

Yes. Teammates come back for your approval, and every task shows a live progress trail of what was done.

### Where does my data go?

Your knowledge bases and task history stay within your workspace. Data for Indian deployments is stored in India and encrypted at rest and in transit. Your teammates use the connected apps you authorise to carry out tasks.
