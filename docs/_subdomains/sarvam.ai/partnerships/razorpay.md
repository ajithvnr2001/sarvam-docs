# Sarvam partners with Razorpay to bring voice-first commerce to India

Combining Sarvam's agentic stack with Razorpay's payments infrastructure to power the next generation of conversational commerce.

![Sarvam x Razorpay - Voice-first commerce for India](https://assets.sarvam.ai/tr:q-70,f-auto,dpr-auto/assets/pages/partnerships/razorpay-sarvam.png)

We are excited to announce our partnership with Razorpay, India's omnichannel payments and banking platform, to power the next generation of voice-first, conversational commerce. By combining Sarvam's models and agentic stack with Razorpay's payments infrastructure, customers can now discover products, place orders, and complete payments simply by conversing in their language. 

## What makes this different

India has over 950 million internet users and more than 450 million digital payment users on UPI. Yet only around 200 million people currently shop online. The gap is not access: it is experience.

### Voice-first payments

Customers tell an AI assistant what they want in the language of their choice and complete the full payment within the same conversation. No typing, no app switching, no English-first menus.

### Multilingual by default

Sarvam's models support Hindi, Hinglish, and a growing set of Indian languages. Every interaction feels natural to the user, not like a translation of an English-first interface.

### Agentic checkout

The agent handles the entire journey, from understanding the request to placing the order to completing payment through Razorpay's infrastructure, powered by NPCI's UPI Reserve Pay.

## Where it is  
live today

The partnership is already running across two real-world surfaces, with more on the way.

### Indus and direct commerce pilots

Voice-first commerce is live on Indus, Sarvam's chat platform, and in an early pilot for The Derma Co's website. Customers can discover products and complete purchases just by speaking in the language they are most comfortable with.

### Razorpay Agent Studio

Sarvam's stack is integrated directly into Razorpay's Agent Studio. Any developer or business building on the Razorpay ecosystem can deploy AI agents that interact with customers in Indian languages, significantly lowering the bar to multilingual commerce.

## What we are  
building together

Sarvam brings the language intelligence. Razorpay brings the payments rails. Together, we are building the foundation for AI-native commerce across India.

### Expanding

More commerce surfaces

Expanding voice-first checkout beyond current pilots to more businesses and platforms building on Razorpay.

### In progress

Deeper language support

Broadening multilingual support across more Indian languages for both the agentic experience and the payments flow.

### Platform

Agent Studio capabilities

More tools for developers in Razorpay's ecosystem to build and deploy multilingual AI agents for their own commerce use cases.

### Mission

Commerce for the next 250M

The goal is to reach Indians who are online but not yet shopping online, by making commerce feel as natural as a conversation.

This is just the beginning of what conversational commerce can look like in India.
