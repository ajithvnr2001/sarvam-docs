We are excited to announce our partnership with YCP India to accelerate enterprise AI adoption across India. Together, we are pairing Sarvam's full-stack sovereign AI platform with YCP's deep cross-sector expertise and execution muscle, helping enterprises identify the highest-impact use cases and capture the full value of AI. 

## Why this matters for  
enterprise AI in India

As foundational models mature, access to technology is no longer the bottleneck. The real differentiator is the ability to apply that technology effectively, at scale, inside enterprises with their own operating realities, regulatory requirements, and legacy systems.

### Full-stack sovereign AI platform

Sarvam brings capabilities spanning language, voice, and multimodal systems, designed for the specific needs of Indian enterprises, including regulatory considerations and cost-efficient deployment at scale.

### Deep cross-sector domain expertise

YCP brings extensive digital transformation experience, rigorous execution capabilities, strategy advisory, and long-standing client relationships across global markets.

### From experimentation to deployment

Most enterprises today are running fragmented AI initiatives with limited business value to show for it. The shift is from scattered pilots to structured, organisation-wide deployment anchored in real outcomes.

## How we are  
working together

The partnership focuses on three areas that together give enterprises a clear path from AI ambition to AI impact.

### Identifying high-impact use cases

We work with clients to prioritise opportunities grounded in business value rather than experimentation, aligning AI initiatives with enterprise strategy, operating models, and existing technology landscapes so every deployment connects to a measurable outcome.

### Co-delivering integrated solutions

Sarvam's stack comes together with YCP's domain and functional expertise to deliver solutions tailored to each client's sector, workflows, and goals, ensuring enterprises get both the technology depth and the business context they need.

### Joint execution on the ground

YCP teams work alongside Sarvam engineers as domain-informed delivery partners, supporting implementation, change management, and rapid value realisation. This shared accountability is what turns AI projects into AI outcomes.
