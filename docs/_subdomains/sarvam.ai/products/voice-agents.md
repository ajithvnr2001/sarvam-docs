## Frequently asked questions.

### What is Sarvam's Voice Agents Platform?

Sarvam's Voice Agents Platform is our conversational AI platform that enables enterprises to deploy intelligent, human-like agents.

### How quickly can I deploy an agent?

You can launch your agent workflow in minutes. Our intuitive interface allows you to customize every detail - write instructions, link your knowledge base, connect tools, and let agents adapt naturally with voice, language, and context. No complex coding required.

### What languages does it support?

Sarvam's Voice Agents Platform supports all major Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, and more. Our AI models are specifically trained on Indian languages and dialects for natural, contextually appropriate conversations.

### Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes, absolutely. Sarvam's Voice Agents Platform integrates seamlessly with telephony systems, CRM platforms, databases, and other business tools. You can automate both inbound and outbound calls, trigger event-driven campaigns, and export data to your existing visualization tools for deeper analysis.

### What kind of analytics do I get?

Our platform provides comprehensive analytics on agent performance, call logs, and conversation insights. You can track key metrics, uncover patterns in customer interactions, and export data seamlessly to your existing visualization tools for deeper analysis and reporting.

### How much do voice agents cost?

From ₹3.50 a minute on pay as you go, down to ₹3.00 on Business, with custom Enterprise pricing for large volumes. The per-minute rate is end to end and includes the models. See every plan, add-on, and rate limit at sarvam.ai/products/voice-agents/pricing.
