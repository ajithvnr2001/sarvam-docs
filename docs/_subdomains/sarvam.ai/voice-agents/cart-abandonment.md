## Start the conversation that  
completes the order

Emails and messages depend on the customer clicking through and finding the answer themselves. Voice calling gives them the answer in the moment, while they're still deciding.

A Sarvam Voice Agent can explain a product, confirm delivery timelines, clarify returns or help with a failed payment, so the customer gets a direct answer without searching for it.

## Voice recovery that fits  
into your commerce stack

Connect Sarvam Voice Agents to the systems that hold the information needed during a recovery call.

### Commerce and catalogues

Commerce and checkout systems, product catalogues, and inventory tell the agent what the customer was buying and whether it's available.

### CRM and customer data

Customer records and past interactions identify the caller and shape how the conversation goes.

### Payments and discounts

Payment gateways and discount or promotion engines let the agent troubleshoot a failed payment or apply an approved incentive.

### Orders and handoff

Order systems confirm delivery timelines and record each call outcome. Complex or high-value calls go to a person, with the cart context intact.
