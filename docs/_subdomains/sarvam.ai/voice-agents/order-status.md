## Built for calls  
that change direction

The conversation can shift from an order status question to a cancellation, refund or address change, switch between Hindi and English, or cover more than one order. Sarvam Voice Agents keep the order context through every turn.

No order number?

### Start with what they know

The agent can search recent purchases and ask for a follow-up when several orders match.

More than one system?

### Bring the context together

An order may be confirmed in one system, fulfilled in another and tracked by a third. The agent can query each one during the conversation.

More than one issue?

### Stay in the same call

A customer can ask about a delayed shipment, then follow up on an earlier return without starting again.

## Tracks the order  
across your stack

Order details sit across your CRM, OMS, carrier and payments provider. Connect them as tools and the Voice Agent can check each one during the call.

### Orders and fulfilment

Find what was ordered and whether it has been confirmed, picked, packed or dispatched.

### Customer context

Identify the caller and use their order history to find the right purchase even when they do not have an order number.

### Delivery and payments

Check carrier updates, failed delivery attempts, payment status and refunds.

### Actions and handoffs

Update connected systems, complete permitted actions and bring in a person when the workflow requires one.
