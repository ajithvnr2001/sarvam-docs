## AI for all from India

We're building toward a future where AI is widely accessible to everyone in India. With a world-class team of researchers and experts, we're dedicated to developing AI that gets India. What excites us is the chance to build and shape this technology to reflect how the country thinks, speaks, reasons, and solves problems.

## We are building for India

We want India to embrace the most important technological shift of our time with confidence and control. Our ambition is to build foundational components and apply them to the country's unique needs. To this end, we've built a full-stack AI platform, with everything developed, deployed, and governed entirely in India.

## We are building for Everyday uses

We believe the real value of our research work will come from everyday use. That means translating our research into compelling products and applications that can be put to work by enterprises, governments and developers.

## We are building for the Community

Sovereign AI is a nationwide, collective effort. With AI opening the door to a new builder era, we want to help catalyze momentum in India. It starts by powering the ecosystem with cost-efficient, high-quality models and tools, so developers, researchers, builders, and startups can create with full agency. The goal is to enable creation at scale by putting state-of-the-art AI in the hands of the community.

##  Our founders 

Sarvam was founded by Dr. Vivek Raghavan and Dr. Pratyush Kumar in August 2023. Vivek brings experience building India's digital public infrastructure, while Pratyush has led the country's open-source AI efforts across Indian languages.
