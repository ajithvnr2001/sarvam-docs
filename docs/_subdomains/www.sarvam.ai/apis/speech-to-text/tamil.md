## Your questions, answered

### How accurate is Tamil speech to text?

Saaras v3 achieves industry-leading word error rates for Tamil, handling agglutinative morphology, grantha consonants, and compound characters with high accuracy.

### Does it handle Tanglish code-mixing?

Yes. Saaras v3 handles natural Tamil-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or dropped words between languages.

### Can I use Tamil STT for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms latency for Tamil, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### Does it understand Tamil dialects?

Saaras v3 is trained on diverse Tamil speech data covering Chennai Tamil, Madurai Tamil, Sri Lankan Tamil, and other regional variations.

### What audio formats are supported?

10+ formats including MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC, M4A, AMR, WMA, and WebM. Streaming API supports WAV and raw PCM at 16kHz.
