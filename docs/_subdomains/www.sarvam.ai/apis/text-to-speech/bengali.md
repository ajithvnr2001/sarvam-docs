## Your questions, answered

### How many Bengali voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers multiple Bengali voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control and temperature adjustment.

### Does it handle Benglish code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 handles natural Bengali-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or unnatural pauses between languages.

### How does it handle Bengali conjunct consonants?

Our deep grapheme-to-phoneme model correctly processes Bengali's complex conjunct consonant forms with high accuracy across literary and colloquial text.

### Can I use Bengali TTS for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Bengali, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Bengali TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz.
