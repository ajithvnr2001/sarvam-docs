## Your questions, answered

### How many Hindi voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers 35+ Hindi voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control (0.5x to 2x) and temperature adjustment for fine-tuned output.

### Does it handle Hinglish code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 was trained on natural Hindi-English code-switching patterns. It transitions seamlessly between languages within the same sentence without accent shifts or unnatural pauses.

### What about Hindi abbreviations and numbers?

The text preprocessor handles Hindi numerals, currency (₹), dates, phone numbers, and common abbreviations automatically. Numbers are spoken naturally in context.

### Can I use Hindi TTS for real-time voice agents?

Absolutely. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Hindi, making it ideal for real-time voice agents, IVR systems, and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Hindi TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz depending on quality requirements.
