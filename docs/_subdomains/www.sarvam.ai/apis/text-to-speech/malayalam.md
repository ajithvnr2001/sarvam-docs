## Your questions, answered

### How many Malayalam voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers multiple Malayalam voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control and temperature adjustment.

### Does it handle Manglish code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 handles natural Malayalam-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or unnatural pauses between languages.

### How does it handle Malayalam compound words?

Our morpheme-aware segmentation model accurately handles Malayalam's extremely long compound words, correctly segmenting and pronouncing complex forms.

### Can I use Malayalam TTS for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Malayalam, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Malayalam TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz.
