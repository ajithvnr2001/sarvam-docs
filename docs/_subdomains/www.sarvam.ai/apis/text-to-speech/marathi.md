## Your questions, answered

### How many Marathi voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers multiple Marathi voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control and temperature adjustment.

### Does it handle Minglish code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 handles natural Marathi-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or unnatural pauses between languages.

### How does it handle Marathi schwa deletion?

Our context-aware model is trained on millions of Marathi utterances to correctly handle schwa deletion patterns that rules-based systems can't predict.

### Can I use Marathi TTS for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Marathi, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Marathi TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz.
