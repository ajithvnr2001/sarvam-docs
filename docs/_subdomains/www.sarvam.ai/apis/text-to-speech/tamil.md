## Your questions, answered

### How many Tamil voices are available?

Bulbul v3 offers multiple Tamil voices with different characteristics: conversational, authoritative, warm, dynamic. Each supports pace control and temperature adjustment.

### Does it handle Tanglish code-switching?

Yes. Bulbul v3 handles natural Tamil-English code-switching seamlessly without accent shifts or unnatural pauses between languages.

### How does it handle Tamil script complexities?

Our model correctly processes Tamil's agglutinative morphology, grantha consonants, and compound characters with high accuracy.

### Can I use Tamil TTS for real-time voice agents?

Yes. Our WebSocket streaming API delivers sub-250ms first byte latency for Tamil, ideal for real-time voice agents and conversational AI.

### What audio formats are supported for Tamil TTS?

All 8 formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, OPUS, FLAC, PCM, MULAW, and ALAW. Sample rates from 8kHz to 24kHz.
