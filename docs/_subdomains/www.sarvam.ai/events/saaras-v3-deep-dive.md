## Event content

We hosted a webinar on Saaras V3, Sarvam's Speech-to-Text model for Indian languages, to help you power your Voice AI use cases with Indian speech-to-text. We walked through how Saaras V3 handles real Indian speech, and what it takes to ship production-ready Voice AI with it.

## What was covered

  * How ASR fits into the broader Voice AI stack, across transcription, dictation, voice agents, and call analytics
  * What makes Saaras V3 work for Indian speech: language coverage, code-mixing, regional accents, and noisy audio
  * Why diarization matters in call centres and meetings, where separating speakers shapes context and intent
  * Streaming vs batch transcription, and the tradeoffs between latency, accuracy, and product experience
  * How Saaras V3 is running in production across customer support, fintech, and consumer apps
