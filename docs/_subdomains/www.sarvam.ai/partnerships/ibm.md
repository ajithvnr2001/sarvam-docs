# IBM and Sarvam bring sovereign AI to mission-critical systems across India

IBM's sovereign core meets Sarvam's India-built AI stack to help governments and regulated enterprises build and deploy AI securely, within the environments they already rely on.

IBM's sovereign core infrastructure and Sarvam's Sovereign AI stack come together to help governments and regulated enterprises move AI from experimentation to production, securely and within the systems they already operate. 

## Bringing sovereign infrastructure  
and AI together

Governments and regulated enterprises need AI that runs securely within infrastructure they control, meets their data residency and governance requirements, and can operate at the scale of their mission-critical systems. 

The partnership combines IBM's sovereign core infrastructure with Sarvam's Sovereign AI stack. IBM brings enterprise-grade sovereign compute and platform capabilities, along with decades of delivery across central and state government programmes. Sarvam brings its full-stack AI platform, with reasoning, language and voice models built in India. 

Together, the combined offering is purpose-built for the public sector and regulated enterprises, where AI needs to work within existing environments while meeting India's data residency and governance requirements. 

## AI for citizen services  
and enterprise workflows

The partnership powers use cases across citizen services and the systems organisations already operate.

Government-to-citizen services can be delivered at population scale, with citizens served in their own language, including by voice. A citizen can access a government service or resolve a grievance through a phone call, in the language they are most comfortable using. 

For organisations, the combination provides a way to bring these AI capabilities into the systems and workflows they already operate, spanning document processing and administrative work. 

AI runs on IBM's sovereign core, so government and enterprise data stays within infrastructure they control, meeting India's data residency and governance requirements. 

## From pilots to production

Moving AI from a pilot into production requires organisations to work through the technical, operational and governance requirements of deploying it within their existing environments. 

The IBM GovTech AI Innovation Center in Lucknow will serve as the partnership's incubation and demonstration hub. Government departments, public sector organisations and enterprises can use the center to test solutions and work through the requirements of moving AI from pilots into production. 

Together, IBM and Sarvam will help governments and regulated enterprises move from AI experimentation to production systems that serve citizens every day.
