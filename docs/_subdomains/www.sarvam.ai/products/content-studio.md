## Frequently asked questions

### Which languages can I dub into?

Content Studio supports dubbing across multiple languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Assamese and English. You can create multiple language versions from a single upload.

### Can I keep the original speaker's voice when dubbing?

Yes. Voice cloning preserves the original speaker's vocal identity across language versions. You can also choose from Sarvam's ready-made voices when you do not need to retain the original voice.

### Does Content Studio support code-mixed speech?

Yes. Content Studio can handle code-mixed speech such as Hindi and English or Telugu and English within the same project, including words and sentences that switch between languages. You can review and edit everything in the same workspace.

### Can Content Studio handle videos with multiple speakers?

Yes. You can set the number of speakers or let Sarvam detect them. Each speaker is separated so their voice remains distinct in the dubbed version.

### Can I edit a dub after it is generated?

Yes. Review the transcript and translation line by line, edit individual sections and regenerate only the parts you want to change.

### Can I control how the translation sounds?

Yes. Choose a translation style based on your content, from formal language to more conversational styles. You can also leave it on Auto and let Sarvam choose the appropriate style.

### How do I create a cloned voice?

Record a short live voice sample in Sarvam Studio and preview the cloned voice before saving it. Once created, the voice can be reused for dubbing, narration and text to speech across projects.

### Can I clone any voice?

You should only clone a voice that you have the rights and consent to use. Explicit consent is required before a cloned voice can be saved.

### What files can I upload for dubbing?

In Sarvam Studio, you can upload video or audio files including MP4, MOV, MP3 and WAV. Uploads are currently capped at 2 GB per file.

### Does Content Studio support lip-sync?

Yes. Lip-sync is available for select projects and helps align the video with the dubbed audio for a more natural viewing experience. Contact us to explore lip-sync for your use case.
