## Your questions, answered.

### What is Sarvam Edge?

Sarvam Edge is our suite of speech AI models that run directly on the device, with no internet connection required. It includes three models: Saaras for speech recognition and Mayura for translation, both supporting 22 Indian languages, and Bulbul for text-to-speech across 11 Indian languages.

### Is it as good as your cloud AI?

The edge models are designed to match cloud accuracy within a few percentage points. Mayura achieves translation quality on par with models four times its size. For most real-world voice use cases, users notice the speed improvement long before they notice any difference in quality.

### Which languages does it support?

Saaras and Mayura support 22 Indian languages for speech recognition and translation. Bulbul supports 11 Indian languages for voice output. You don't need separate model versions for different languages.

### What devices can run Sarvam Edge?

Any modern smartphone, laptop, car head unit, or wearable. The models are small enough to run on mid-range Android and iOS devices and are optimized for the CPU, GPU, and NPU chips that ship in today's hardware.

### Does Sarvam Edge work without an internet connection?

Yes. All inference runs entirely on the device. Sarvam Edge works on factory floors, in moving vehicles, and in areas with no signal. There is no fallback to a cloud API and no dependency on network availability.

### How does Sarvam Edge handle data privacy?

No audio, text, or user data leaves the device at any point during inference. Enterprise policy controls define which models run, on which devices, and under what conditions. A full audit trail with hardware-level attestation is included with every deployment.

### What chipsets does Sarvam Edge support?

Sarvam Edge ships pre-validated model variants for Qualcomm Snapdragon (Hexagon NPU), NVIDIA Jetson and TensorRT, Intel Core Ultra (OpenVINO), and Apple Silicon. Each variant is tuned to the target chipset and re-validates automatically when a model is updated.

### Can models be updated after deployment?

Yes. Models are updated over the air using delta patches. Each patch is validated against every silicon target before rollout. Device makers do not need to release a new app version or perform chipset-level re-integration.

### What is the integration effort for OEMs?

Minimal. Sarvam handles all chipset-level optimisation and delivers pre-validated model variants for your target silicon. Integration guides and reference designs are included. Most OEM partners are running on hardware within weeks of engagement.

### How does on-device AI compare to cloud AI in cost?

Cloud AI charges per API call. At any meaningful scale (millions of users, always-on voice features, high-frequency interactions) per-call costs compound quickly. On-device inference has zero marginal cost after deployment. The economics shift entirely once the models are on the device.

### Is Sarvam Edge open source?

The Sarvam 105B and Sarvam 30B language models are open source under the Apache 2.0 license and available on Hugging Face. The edge runtime, silicon-optimised variants, and enterprise tooling are available through a commercial partnership.
