##  Training methods 

We select the training method during scoping based on the task, the dataset, and the evaluation criteria. 

01 

###  Adaptation 

What it is 

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning with LoRA on an existing model. It updates a subset of parameters and supports shorter iteration cycles. 

* * *

Best for 

Defined tasks with a few thousand high-quality training examples. 

02 

###  Specialisation 

What it is 

Full-weight fine-tuning with alignment against agreed policies and evaluation criteria. 

* * *

Best for 

Production workloads that need a smaller model for a specific domain or task.

### Ownership of trained weights

The contract assigns the trained weights to the customer. The customer can run, modify, or host them.

### No reuse of training data

Training data is isolated to the engagement. It is not pooled with other customers or used to train Sarvam models.

### Training data remains in India

Training runs on infrastructure in India. The customer chooses where to run the weights after delivery.

## Model training FAQs

### Who manages the training infrastructure?

Sarvam manages GPU capacity, cluster configuration, training runs, recovery, alignment, and evaluation.

### What is required to start?

A defined task, a dataset, and acceptance criteria. During scoping, we review data readiness and identify preparation required before training.

### Which base models are supported?

Training currently supports Sarvam 105B, Gemma 3 27B, and selected open-weight models. We choose the base model during scoping based on task requirements and latency constraints.

### How is training data handled?

Training data is isolated to the engagement on Sarvam infrastructure in India. Access, retention, and deletion terms are agreed in writing before transfer. The data is not pooled with other customers or used to train Sarvam models.

### How long does an engagement take?

Adaptation engagements are measured in weeks. Specialisation takes longer. The schedule is set after we review the dataset and evaluation requirements.

### How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on training depth, evaluation requirements, and data readiness. We provide a quote after scoping.
