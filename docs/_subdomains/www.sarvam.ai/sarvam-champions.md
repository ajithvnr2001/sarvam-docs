## Frequently asked questions

### Who can apply to be a Sarvam Champion?

Anyone who has shipped something real on Sarvam or who already runs a credible local developer community. Creators need a technical audience and the ability to demo a build, not just talk about one. City Leads need a track record of running events people actually show up to.

### What is the difference between a Creator and a City Lead?

Creators produce content: tutorials, cookbook recipes, build-alongs, and product feedback. City Leads run one Sarvam event a month in their city, funded and supported by us. You join one track, though Champions do move between them over time.

### Where is the program open?

Anywhere there are builders. City Leads run events in whichever city they are based in, and the Creator track is open regardless of location. Tell us your city when you apply.

### How does event funding work?

Sarvam covers the on-ground cost of every City Lead event. We settle food and catering directly. You arrange the rest, such as standees, photography, and miscellaneous costs, and get reimbursed against a pre-approved budget and receipts. The support covers event costs, not cash prizes.

### Does it cost anything to be a Champion?

No. The program is funded by Sarvam. You should never be paying to run a Sarvam event or to create content for us.
