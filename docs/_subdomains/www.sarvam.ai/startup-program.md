## Frequently asked questions

### How many credits do we get?

Credit allocation depends on your use case, stage, and expected scale. Approved startups receive 6–12 months of API credits tailored to their needs.

### When do credits expire?

Credits are valid for the duration of your program term (6–12 months). Unused credits do not roll over after the program ends.

### Can existing Sarvam users apply?

Yes. If you're already using Sarvam APIs, you can apply to the startup program for additional credits and priority support.

### What happens after credits end?

You transition to standard API pricing. Our team will work with you on a plan that fits your scale and usage patterns.

### Do we need to display "Powered by Sarvam" branding?

Yes. Program participants are expected to display "Powered by Sarvam" in their product. This helps build the ecosystem and is a condition of the program.
