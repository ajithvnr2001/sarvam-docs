## Answer a customer's questions  
before they commit

Customers often ask about eligibility, documentation, how this compares to other lenders, or what happens if they miss a payment. Connect the agent to your loan policies and product catalogue, and it answers in the same call. It stays within approved language, never confirms final approval or guarantees a rate, and transfers the call when a person needs to take over.

## Built around how  
people actually talk

Customers describe their concerns in their own words, and the agent works out the rest.

Natural language

### No fixed script

Customers raise objections in their own words, on rate, tenure, or trust, and the agent responds to the actual concern instead of reading a script.

Handles hesitation

### Room to think

Customers can pause, ask to be called back, or interrupt mid-sentence. The agent detects voicemail and follows up rather than leaving a dead call.

Multilingual conversations

### One offer, any language

Customers can discuss their loan offer in Hindi, Tamil or any of 12 Indian languages your base speaks, with rates, fees and terms explained just as clearly in each.

## Connected to your systems

Connect Sarvam Voice Agents to the systems that hold the information needed during a sales call.

### CRM and customer data

Customer records and product holdings identify the caller and surface which cross-sell offers actually apply to them.

### Loan origination and servicing

The agent pulls the pre-approved offer and terms, and pushes the confirmed details once the customer agrees.

### Collections and repayment history

Repayment behaviour and collections history inform which offers a customer sees and at what terms, so the agent only pitches what they qualify for.

### Payments and disbursal

Once documents are verified, the case hands off to disbursal. Anything outside the agent's scope transfers to a person with full context intact.
