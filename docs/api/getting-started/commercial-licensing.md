# Commercial Licensing

Ask a question|Copy page|[View as Markdown](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/commercial-licensing.md "View this page as plain text")|More actions

This page is for Model APIs.

This page explains how commercial production rights work when you generate audio with **Bulbul v3** , **Content Studio** (Text to Speech, Dubbing, Instant Voice Cloning), or the **Text-to-Speech API**. It covers credits, the Production License under our [End User License Agreement (EULA)](https://www.sarvam.ai/eula), voice-cloning consent, and what happens if you stop using Sarvam later.

**Summary:** You may use exported audio in shipped, sold products, including games, apps, and commercial media, when generation runs on your account and credits are consumed, including from your **₹100 signup credit pool**. No separate production tier or commercial registration step is required beyond normal credit usage and voice-cloning consent.

* * *

## Production License and credits

Sarvam grants a **Production License** when you pay applicable fees for the Software and remain compliant with our [EULA](https://www.sarvam.ai/eula) and [Terms of Service](https://www.sarvam.ai/terms-of-service). On the managed platform, **credit consumption counts as payment** for the generation that produced your Output.

That means:

  * **Signup credits count.** Every new account receives [₹100 in free credits](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/ratelimits). These credits are **not** limited to evaluation-only or non-commercial use. Audio you generate while those credits are consumed carries the same production rights as audio generated from a paid top-up.
  * **Paid top-ups count the same way.** Any credit purchase from the [Billing page](https://dashboard.sarvam.ai/billing) activates production rights for Output generated while those credits are used.
  * **There is no separate Production License SKU.** You do not need a special plan name or add-on beyond having credits available and using the service in compliance with our terms.



The Production License applies to **Output you generate while credits are consumed on your account**. It does not grant rights to Sarvam’s models, voices, software, or trademarks themselves, only to the generated audio (and other Output) assigned to you under the EULA.

* * *

## Minimum top-up or plan

**No minimum purchase is required** beyond normal credit usage.

  * Use your **₹100 signup credits** , or top up any amount from the dashboard when you need more.
  * [Plan tiers](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/ratelimits#plan-overview) (Starter, Pro, Business) mainly affect **rate limits** and **bonus credits** : not whether you may ship commercial Output.
  * Bulbul v3 is billed at [₹30 per 10,000 characters](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/pricing). Generate and export only what your project needs; each billed generation is licensed for production use.



If you need volume discounts, custom billing, or enterprise deployment options, see [Talk to us](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/help).

* * *

## Voice cloning for commercial use

**Instant Voice Cloning** in Content Studio and cloned voices used through the Instant Voice Cloning API follow the same commercial rules as preset Sarvam voices, with one extra requirement: **you must have the rights to clone the voice**.

### What you must do

  1. **Complete consent in the dashboard.** Before saving a clone, you must check the mandatory consent box confirming you have the rights to record, process, clone, and use that voice to generate new content. See [Instant Voice Cloning overview](https://docs.sarvam.ai/creative-voice-cloning) and [Creating a Clone](https://docs.sarvam.ai/creative-cloning-create).
  2. **Clone only voices you are authorized to use.** Self-voiced, consent-based clones (for example, your own voice for an in-game character) are supported when you hold those rights.
  3. **Generate final assets on your account.** Export or download audio after generation runs on your org with credits consumed, the same as any other Bulbul v3 output.



### What you do **not** need

  * No separate commercial registration form for cloned voices
  * No enterprise plan requirement for Content Studio voice cloning (enterprise-only cloning rules apply to **Conversations / voice agents** custom voices. See [Voice & language settings](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/voice-language#voice-cloning-enterprise))



You may not use Sarvam to impersonate individuals without consent, generate deepfakes, or violate applicable law, including voice personality and data-protection requirements. See the [EULA License Restrictions](https://www.sarvam.ai/eula).

* * *

## Rights after you stop using Sarvam

**Yes: you retain commercial rights to Output you have already exported** , subject to the EULA.

Under our EULA:

  * Sarvam **assigns to you** all rights, title, and interest in **Output** (generated responses, including audio files) that you receive from the Software.
  * The Production License for Output generated while you paid applicable fees and complied with the Agreement is **perpetual** for that Output. You do not lose the right to use already-exported files in your shipped product if you later cancel, stop topping up, or close your account.



You remain responsible for how you use that Output (accuracy, consent, third-party rights, and applicable law).

* * *

## Common commercial use cases

Use case| Licensed for commercial shipment?  
---|---  
Bulbul v3 API audio in a paid app or game| Yes, when generated on your account with credits consumed  
Content Studio Text to Speech export (Sarvam or cloned voice)| Yes, same conditions  
Dubbing export for a commercial video or game localization| Yes, same conditions  
Prototype audio generated before you had credits| No. Generate final assets on your account before shipping  
Re-selling Sarvam voices or models as a standalone product| No. See EULA restrictions  
  
* * *

For account-specific confirmation (for example, before a major commercial release), email [support@sarvam.ai](mailto:support@sarvam.ai) with your org ID and use case.
