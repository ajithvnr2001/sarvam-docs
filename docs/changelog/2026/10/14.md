## Bulbul V3 End of Life for Voice Agents

[Voice Agents](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Voice%20Agents)

Bulbul V3 reaches end of life on Voice Agents on **November 10, 2026**. Switch every agent that speaks with Bulbul V3 to Bulbul V4 before that date. In the agent’s **Settings** , set the model under **Speaking** to **Bulbul V4** and pick a voice. To keep a voice you use today, find its Bulbul V4 name in [Voice name changes from V3 to V4](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/voice-language#voice-name-changes-from-v3-to-v4). This change applies to Voice Agents only. Bulbul V3 stays available on the [Text-to-Speech API](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/models/bulbul).

## Volume Adjustment for Voice Agents

[Voice Agents](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Voice%20Agents)

You can now control how loud your voice agent sounds. A new **Volume** slider in the agent’s voice settings (Sarvam / Bulbul voices) sets the loudness of the agent’s speech, from `0.7` (quieter) to `2.0` (louder), with `1.0` as the natural level. The volume is stored with the agent’s voice configuration and applied on every call, so it can be set once and manipulated later. See [Speakers & voice](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/voice-language#volume).
