## GLM-5.3, Gemma 4 31B, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, and the Responses API Are Generally Available

[Model APIs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Model%20APIs)

[`POST /v2/chat/completions`](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference/chat/chat-completions-v2) and [`POST /v2/responses`](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference/responses/create) are now generally available with [GLM-5.3](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/models/openweight/glm-5-3), [Gemma 4 31B](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/models/openweight/gemma-4-31b), and [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/models/openweight/deepseek-v4.1-flash), alongside `sarvam-105b`. No beta whitelisting is required for these endpoints or models. See [Beta APIs and Models](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference/beta-apis) for anything still in beta.
