This page is for Cowork.

## One workspace. Every workflow.

Describe what you want done, and Cowork does it: drafting documents, pulling data from your connected systems, sending emails, summarising reports, and running multi-step tasks on its own. No code required.

![Cowork Home dashboard with a central prompt bar and a sidebar with Connectors, Skills, Agents, Scheduled Tasks, and Knowledge Bases.](https://docs.sarvam.ai/_fern-img/e57c0ba0f3267a2822d3455598ccdf1ff254ee87519843ce24b09442ce20bde3.webp)

The Cowork workspace: one prompt bar, one sidebar, everything you need to put AI to work.

## What it is

**Cowork** is an agentic workspace where you work with AI for ad-hoc tasks and build agents for recurring workflows. Through Connectors, your agents can also read from and take action inside the external systems you already use, such as Gmail, Slack, GitHub, and Notion. You can work with it live, or hand a repeating job to an agent that runs on a schedule or inside the channels where your team already works.

[BuildCreate agents in plain English, give them connectors, skills, and knowledge, then run them on demand or on a schedule.](https://docs.sarvam.ai/cowork/build/creating-an-agent)[Use casesWorked examples: email triage, morning briefings, an internal helpdesk, and lead follow-up.](https://docs.sarvam.ai/cowork/use-cases/overview)

## Where to find Cowork

Cowork lives in the Sarvam platform. Log in with your Sarvam account and open **Cowork** to start working:

[Open CoworkLog in at indus.sarvam.ai/indus to put AI to work, build agents, and automate your workflows.](https://indus.sarvam.ai/indus)

## Two ways to work: Chat and Agents

The same workspace supports both one-off and recurring work. What changes is whether you build something reusable.

You want to…| Use| How it works  
---|---|---  
Get a one-off task done now| **Chat**|  Type a task in the prompt bar and get an immediate response  
Standardise a task you repeat| **Agents**|  Build a reusable worker once, then run it on demand or on a schedule  
  
**Rule of thumb:** if you will only do it once, chat. If you will do it again, build an agent.

## What you can build

A few things teams do with Cowork on day one: generate documents and reports, draft and send email, explain dense reports, prepare recurring briefings, run an internal helpdesk, and qualify and follow up on leads.

See [Use cases](https://docs.sarvam.ai/cowork/use-cases/overview) for the full list and who each is for.
