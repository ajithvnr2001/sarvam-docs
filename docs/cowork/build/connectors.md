# Connectors

Ask a question|Copy page|[View as Markdown](https://docs.sarvam.ai/cowork/build/connectors.md "View this page as plain text")|More actions

This page is for Cowork.

**Connectors** link your agents to the external systems and websites you already use: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, Linear, Notion, and more. Once connected, an agent can read information from these systems and take action inside them.

![Cowork Connectors page showing a searchable gallery of connectors such as Apollo, Asana, Ashby, Calendly, Circleback, Cloudflare, Datadog, Deel, Excalidraw, Fireflies, GitHub, Google Workspace, and HeyGen, with Google Workspace and Excalidraw marked Connected.](https://docs.sarvam.ai/_fern-img/ab253acdd330560a0db5e267d371bdac7a2896f79f15ef2fb576ea2cbca04747.webp)

The Connectors gallery: link Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, and more to your workspace.

## Authorising a connector

Each connector needs to be linked to your account and authorised before use. You will be guided through a secure OAuth flow where you sign in to the external service and grant permission. Once authorised, the connector is added to your **Chat** tab and is available for your agents to use.

## Using a connector in an agent

In the **Agent Builder** , add or remove the connectors a given agent can use, keeping each agent scoped to the tools it actually needs. See [Create custom agents](https://docs.sarvam.ai/cowork/build/creating-an-agent).

**Great for:** any task that reaches beyond the prompt bar into your real accounts: reading and drafting email, checking your calendar, fetching files, or posting to team channels.
