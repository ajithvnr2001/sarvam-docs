# Knowledge Bases

Ask a question|Copy page|[View as Markdown](https://docs.sarvam.ai/cowork/build/knowledge-bases.md "View this page as plain text")|More actions

This page is for Cowork.

A **Knowledge Base** is a collection of your own documents and reference material that an agent can draw on: policies, product docs, past reports, FAQs, contracts. Add your files once, and any connected agent can search and cite that material while it works, so its answers come from your source of truth rather than general knowledge.

![Cowork Knowledge bases empty state reading 'Add your first knowledge source', with 'Add knowledge source' and 'New knowledge base' options.](https://docs.sarvam.ai/_fern-img/e868777a7fb8ce587573c19af6437da8f99edce1ebb2cfcbc32c931d70d1160d.webp)

Knowledge Bases: add your documents once so agents answer from your source of truth.

## Add a knowledge source

Create a knowledge base, add your files as knowledge sources, and connect it to the agents that should use it. From then on, those agents search and cite the material while they work.

**Great for:** support and helpdesk agents, policy and compliance questions, and any task where accuracy against your own documents matters.
