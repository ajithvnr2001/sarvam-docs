# Why Voice Agents

Ask a question|Copy page|[View as Markdown](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/why-voice-agents.md "View this page as plain text")|More actions

This page is for Voice Agents.

**If you are building a voice agent for customers in India, Sarvam Voice Agents gives you the whole stack in one platform:** speech recognition, reasoning, and speech synthesis built for Indian languages, plus telephony, testing, outbound campaigns, and analytics. Pricing is per minute, from ₹3.50 on pay as you go down to ₹3.15 on Growth, and ₹3 on Business (coming soon). See [Pricing](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing).

## What you get in one platform

  * **11 Indian languages plus English.** Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, and Assamese, including code-mixed speech, alphanumerics, and proper nouns. See [Overview](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/overview).
  * **Sarvam’s own models, self-hosted by Sarvam.** Saaras v4 for speech recognition, Bulbul v3 and v4 for speech synthesis, and `sarvam-105b-conversations` for real-time dialogue, orchestrated for you. See [Models](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/models).
  * **Data residency in India.** All data, including any PII, stays within India.
  * **A run-time built for real phone calls.** Interruptions and barge-in, language switching mid-call, voicemail detection, hold, long silences, and correct pronunciation of names and numbers are handled out of the box. See [Run-time](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/run-time).
  * **Telephony included.** [Rent an Indian number from Sarvam](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/deploy/telephony/rent-from-sarvam) or [bring your own provider](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/deploy/telephony/bring-your-own). The same agent also runs on WhatsApp, web, and API.
  * **Test before you go live.** Run [simulated test calls](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/tests) against scripted scenarios.
  * **Outbound at scale and analytics.** Run [campaigns](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/deploy/campaigns), then read call logs, [analytics, and custom boards](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/monitor/overview).
  * **Build from your AI assistant.** Connect Claude, Cursor, or VS Code over the [MCP server](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/mcp) to build, test, and deploy agents.



## Build it yourself vs Sarvam Voice Agents

A production voice agent needs more than a speech API. Here is what you would assemble yourself, and what Voice Agents already includes.

You need| Build it yourself| Sarvam Voice Agents  
---|---|---  
Speech recognition for Indian languages| Choose and integrate an ASR provider| Saaras v4, included  
Reasoning (LLM)| Choose, host, and prompt a model| `sarvam-105b-conversations`, routed automatically  
Indian-language voices| Choose and integrate a TTS provider| Bulbul v3 and v4, included  
Turn-taking, barge-in, orchestration| Run a framework such as LiveKit or Pipecat and tune it| [Harness and run-time](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/concepts/harness), included  
Phone numbers and telephony| Contract and integrate a telephony provider| Rent a number for ₹59 per 30 days, or bring your own  
Testing| Build your own test harness| Simulated test runs at ₹2 per run  
Outbound campaigns| Build scheduling and dialling| [Campaigns](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/deploy/campaigns), included  
Analytics| Build logging and dashboards| [Analytics and boards](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/monitor/overview), included  
Data residency| Check each vendor separately| All data stays in India  
Billing| Several vendors and invoices| One per-minute rate from one credit balance  
  
## What it costs

Plan| Voice agent rate  
---|---  
Pay as you go| ₹3.50 per minute  
Pro| ₹3.25 per minute  
Growth| ₹3.15 per minute  
Business (coming soon)| ₹3.00 per minute  
Enterprise (2 lakh minutes a month and above)| Custom, on committed volume  
  
Self-serve plans are prepaid from your credit balance. Enterprise is postpaid and invoiced monthly. For example, 10,000 minutes a month costs ₹35,000 on pay as you go. Telephony is billed separately. See [Pricing](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing) for plan fees, telephony rates, rate limits, and a cost estimate by volume.

## When to use the APIs instead

Use Sarvam’s [speech-to-text](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/api-guides-tutorials/speech-to-text/overview), [text-to-speech](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/api-guides-tutorials/text-to-speech/overview), and [chat](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/api-guides-tutorials/chat-completion/overview) APIs directly if you already run your own orchestration (for example [LiveKit](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/integration/build-voice-agent-with-live-kit) or [Pipecat](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/integration/build-voice-agent-with-pipecat)) and want full control of every component. For everything else, start with Voice Agents.
